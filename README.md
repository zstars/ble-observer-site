# BLE Observer site

Public pages for the BLE Observer Android app: the app page (`index.html`, with its images in `app/`), the privacy policy (`privacy/`), the article (`article/`), the guides (`find-lost-earbuds/`, `export-ble-to-excel/`, `scan-ble-beacons/`; Spanish under `es/`, built with `tools/gen_guide_pages.py` in the app repo) and the foreground-service demo video for Google Play (`fgs-demo.mp4`). `app/` only redirects to the root, for old links. `404.html` is the not-found page, linking to every page. `google03d971c0141f7b35.html` verifies the site in Google Search Console: keep it.

The sources live in the app repository, in `play/`; copy them here when they change.
