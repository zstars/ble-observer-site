# BLE Observer site

Public pages for the BLE Observer Android app: the app page (`index.html`, with its images in `app/`), the privacy policy (`privacy/`), the articles (`article/`) and the foreground-service demo video for Google Play (`fgs-demo.mp4`). `app/` only redirects to the root, for old links. `google03d971c0141f7b35.html` verifies the site in Google Search Console: keep it.

The sources live in the app repository, in `play/`; copy them here when they change.
